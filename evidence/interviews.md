# Student Research & Pain Point Evidence (5 Interviews)

### Interview 1: Deepak Maurya (B.Tech CSE, Final Year)
- "I send the exact same resume to every on-campus company. I know I should tailor it, but doing it manually for 15 companies takes hours."
- Key Pain: High time cost of tailoring resumes for frequent campus recruitment drives.

### Interview 2: Yashi Chaudhary (B.Tech IT, 3rd Year)
- "When a company announces an on-campus drive 2 days in advance, I panic because I don't know which specific skills from their JD I need to revise first."
- Key Pain: Lack of a fast, prioritized study and revision roadmap based on the specific job description.

### Interview 3: Manish Singh (B.Tech CSE, Final Year)
- "Generic online ATS checkers just give a vanity score like '62%' but never tell me what practical bullet points or projects to add to fix it."
- Key Pain: Existing tools provide useless percentages without concrete, actionable fixes.

### Interview 4: Mahi (B.Tech CSE, 4th Year)
- "I struggled to answer 'Why are you suitable for this role?' during my last interview because I hadn't mapped my projects to their job description."
- Key Pain: Disconnect between personal projects and JD requirements during interviews.

### Interview 5: Yash Agrahari (B.Tech ECE, 3rd Year)
- "Most AI tools generate long essays. I just need 5 bullet points to revise and 3 interview questions this specific company will ask."
- Key Pain: Information overload and lack of concise, actionable technical preparation.
